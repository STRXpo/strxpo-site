Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 7, 2026  ·  Last Updated: August 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Short-Term Rental Expo & Conference Group, LLC, doing business as STRXpo ("STRXpo," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you visit strxpo.com (the "Site") or interact with our forms, communications, and services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

STRXpo's Site includes multiple forms through which you may voluntarily submit information, including pre-registration, exhibitor and sponsorship prospectus requests, speaker applications, awards nominations, venue bid submissions, media inquiries, and general contact requests. Depending on the specific form, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Company or organization name
  • Your role or category within the vacation rental industry (e.g., host, property manager, exhibitor)
  • Free-text information you choose to provide, such as a nomination narrative, a speaking topic and background, or a message describing your inquiry

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit the Site, we and our service providers automatically collect certain information through cookies and similar technologies, including:

  • IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Browser type and device information
  • Pages visited, time spent on pages, and referring/exit pages
  • General usage and interaction data

This information is collected via Google Analytics. See our Cookie Policy for details.

1.3 Information From Third Parties

If you purchase a ticket to STRXpo, that transaction is processed entirely by Eventbrite, a third-party ticketing platform, under Eventbrite's own privacy policy and terms. Depending on the ticket-purchase flow in place at the time, STRXpo may receive limited information from Eventbrite confirming your ticket type and purchase (such as name and email), for the purpose of event administration and attendee communication.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above for the following purposes:

  • To process and respond to registrations, inquiries, applications, nominations, and bid submissions
  • To communicate with you about STRXpo, including event updates, registration status, and related announcements
  • To evaluate exhibitor, sponsor, speaker, and awards submissions
  • To administer the event itself, including badge/registration processing and attendee communication
  • To analyze and improve the Site's performance and content
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Use

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Site uses cookies via Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the Site. You can control cookie behavior through your browser settings. See our Cookie Policy for full detail on the cookies in use and how to manage them.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, including for the duration of the event planning cycle and a reasonable period thereafter for recordkeeping, unless a longer retention period is required by law. You may request deletion of your information at any time as described in Section 6 below.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, which may include the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time (each Mailchimp communication includes an unsubscribe link)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information in Section 10 below. This section is intended as a general framework; specific rights and procedures (such as those under the California Consumer Privacy Act or other applicable state or international law) should be confirmed with counsel and reflected here prior to publication.

7. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to individuals under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the information below so we can address it.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect the information we collect. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Third-Party Links

The Site may contain links to third-party websites and services, including Eventbrite, sponsor and exhibitor websites, and social media platforms. This Privacy Policy does not apply to those third-party sites, and we encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the Site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
