STRXpo Host of the Year Hosting Platform of the Year Turnover & Staging Company of the Year Property Manager of the Year Property Management Company of the Year Real Estate Agent of the Year Independent QC Company of the Year Exhibitor of the Year Rising Star / Newcomer of the Year Best Guest Experience Innovation Award
