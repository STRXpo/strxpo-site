### Headline
**The Perfect Welcome. One Single Delivery.**

### Body Copy

HGC is the premier single-source food and beverage vendor for the modern short-term rental. Every item a guest eats or drinks during a stay — coffee, tea, bottled water, artisanal snacks, condiments, and curated welcome baskets — is available through one vendor, eliminating the need to piece together a welcome experience from multiple grocery stores and online retailers.

Ordering is entirely flexible. Properties can be stocked à la carte, in any quantity, or through Signature Bundles such as The Coffee Bar Bundle or The Local Snack Box, offered at volume pricing. Each client works directly with a dedicated HGC account manager to curate a signature "Welcome Taste" for their properties, with orders placed on demand or on a recurring schedule.

HGC serves independent hosts and property managers operating up to 50 properties — a deliberate cap that allows every client to be paired with a dedicated, human account manager rather than a call center. HGC does not supply paper goods, cleaning supplies, or toiletries, and does not serve operators above 50 properties, by design — this is a company built for portfolios where guest experience still depends on individual attention.

What makes HGC the single source for guest consumables is the combination of flexibility and personal service. There are no bulk minimums, and every client has a direct line to an actual account manager who can adjust a delivery, build a one-off VIP gift basket, or swap a snack brand on request — a level of attention large-scale suppliers are not built to offer.
