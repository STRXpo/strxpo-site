An Essential Role, Historically Underrepresented

Hosts, property managers, and platforms depend on consistent, reliable turnover service. Despite this, turnover and staging professionals are frequently treated as a service line rather than an industry partner, with limited representation at industry events. Most vacation rental industry events are built around hosts and software, leaving the turnover and cleaning services that make every stay possible without a seat at the table.

STRXpo addresses this directly. Alongside real estate agents and leasing managers, turnover and staging is one of three foundational service categories this event is built around, represented on the main floor.
