1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites function, and to collect information about how a site is used.

3. Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings, including blocking or deleting cookies. You can also opt out of Google Analytics tracking specifically by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Please note that blocking cookies may affect the functionality of the Site.
