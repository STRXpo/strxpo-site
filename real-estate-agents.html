Why This Invitation

The property pipeline behind the industry

Every short-term rental began as a real estate transaction, and every apartment used for short-term rental purposes exists because a leasing manager approved that use. Real estate agents and leasing managers are not adjacent to this industry — they are the property pipeline that makes it possible.

Without agents identifying and closing investment-friendly properties, hosts have nothing to acquire. Without leasing managers establishing and enforcing short-term rental policy at the community level, entire categories of inventory are simply unavailable. This is a foundational relationship, and STRXpo intends to treat it as one.

That is why this invitation extends beyond general attendance. We want real estate agents and leasing managers on our exhibit floor, represented among our sponsors, and featured on our main stage — shaping the conversation, not just attending it.
