The STRXpo Advantage

Uniting the Short-Term Rental Ecosystem

Hosts, property managers, service providers, and technology companies have historically operated in separate channels. STRXpo brings these groups together on a single floor, creating opportunities for meaningful partnerships across the industry.

The Foundation

Three Essential Roles Behind Every Listing

Hosts are often the public face of this industry, but the professionals who prepare properties between stays, the agents who identify investment-ready properties, and the leasing managers who authorize short-term rental use are equally foundational. STRXpo's exhibit floor is designed to represent all three.
