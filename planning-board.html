Help Shape What STRXpo Becomes

The STRXpo Planning Board guides the direction of future events — conference programming, exhibitor and sponsor policy, and the long-term standards this event is built on. Seats on this board are extended by invitation, in recognition of standing and expertise within the industry.

What the Board Does

Setting the Direction for Future Years

Planning Board members participate in shaping future STRXpo events — including conference programming, policy decisions, and the standards that govern exhibitor, sponsor, and attendee experience. This is a continuing role, not a one-time appointment.

Board Membership

A Recognition of Standing in This Industry

Planning Board seats are limited and are extended based on demonstrated expertise and standing within the short-term rental industry. Members are recognized publicly as founding contributors to the direction of this event.
