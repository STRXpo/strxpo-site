FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about STRXpo, including dates, registration, exhibiting, and sponsorship. Certain details remain under confirmation and are indicated accordingly.

The Event
What is STRXpo? +
The first national trade show built specifically for the vacation rental industry — bringing independent hosts, property managers, cleaning and turnover teams, software providers, and booking platforms together in one place, instead of scattered across Facebook groups and general hospitality conferences.
When and where is it? +
Venue TBD
September 2027, in the Orlando / Lake Buena Vista, FL area. The venue is currently being finalized and will be announced upon confirmation. Pre-register to be notified when it is announced. Venue operators may view our open Venue RFB.
Is this the first time STRXpo has run? +
Yes. This is the inaugural year of STRXpo. Our year-one goals are 1,000+ attendees, 75+ exhibiting brands, and 20+ sponsors and partners.
Who organizes STRXpo? +
STRXpo is organized by a team of operators with hands-on experience in hosting, turnover, and property management. Additional background is available on the About page.
Attending
Who should attend? +
Independent and aspiring hosts, real estate agents and apartment community leasing managers, property managers and management companies, cleaning/turnover/staging businesses, software and technology providers, booking platforms, and other industry service providers — see the full breakdown on the Who Attends page.
How much do tickets cost? +
Early-bird: $29  ·  Standard: $49  ·  VIP / All-Access: $99  ·  Single-day: $19.

Duo Early-Bird: $50 for 2 — for co-hosts or business partners registering together during the early-bird window, a savings of $8 versus two individual tickets.

Ticket pricing is structured to remain accessible, as independent and aspiring hosts represent the core audience for this event.
How do I register? +
Full registration has not yet opened. Pre-register with your email and role to be notified as soon as registration becomes available.
