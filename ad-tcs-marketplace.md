### Body Copy

TCS Marketplace is the vacation rental industry's first unified labor network, connecting property managers and hosts with the vetted local professionals who handle turnovers — cleaning, staging, and emergency maintenance — between every checkout and check-in.

The process is automated from end to end. Once a host's booking calendar is connected, TCS generates a Turnover Ticket the moment a guest checks out, complete with a budget and deadline, and pushes it to local, vetted professionals. A pro claims the job, completes host-required tasks, and uploads time-stamped, geo-tagged photos for approval. Payment releases from escrow the moment the property is confirmed ready.

TCS Marketplace is built for mid-scale property managers and growing co-hosts — organizations managing between 5 and 50 doors who are scaling faster than their local labor network can keep up. During its stealth phase, TCS completed over 15,000 turnovers with a 99.4% on-time completion rate, and currently manages turnovers for more than 850 properties in closed beta across three pilot cities.

What makes TCS the single source for turnover labor is accountability. Funds remain in escrow until photo verification is approved, so hosts never pay for an unfinished job, and professionals never chase a late invoice. It replaces the group texts, spreadsheets, and informal payments that turnover coordination has always run on.
