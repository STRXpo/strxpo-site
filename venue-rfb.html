The Process

How the bid process works

Submit Your Bid

Interested venues submit a competitive bid to rfborlando2027@strxpo.com during the open submission window, addressing the requirements and checklist below — including any added incentives you're offering to win the selection.

Review & Compare

Bids are reviewed side by side against event requirements, rates, and the incentives offered. Select venues may be invited to host a site visit or sharpen their offer.

Selection & Contract

The winning bid is selected and a contract executed. The selected venue will be publicly announced as part of STRXpo's official launch announcement.

Bid Checklist

What to include in your bid

A complete, competitive bid should address the following:

  • Available dates and flexibility around September 23–25, 2027 (setup access from Wednesday, September 22)
  • Total exhibit hall / event space square footage and floor plan
  • General session and breakout room capacity and configuration
  • On-site or adjacent hotel room block availability and rates
  • Food & beverage capabilities: daily breakfast (exhibitors, sponsors, and VIP/All-Access attendees, all 3 days), a Friday evening reception with appetizers, soft drinks, and a cash bar, and a Saturday closing dinner — please include per-person catering rates and any minimums
  • Audio-visual, internet, and production capabilities (in-house or preferred vendor)
  • Rental rates, packages, and any applicable service fees
  • Cancellation, rebooking, and force majeure terms
  • Parking, load-in/load-out access, and loading dock details
  • References from prior trade shows or conventions of comparable size
  • Any additional incentives, upgrades, or perks offered to strengthen your bid
