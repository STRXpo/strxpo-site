Two Companies Debut at STRXpo

Ninety-two percent of trade show attendees cite discovering new products and services as their primary reason for attending. This year, two companies will introduce themselves to the industry for the first time, each offering door prizes to early registrants at their booths.

Meet TCS Marketplace Meet Hospitality Guest Consumables
Industry Debut

TCS Marketplace

Turnover · Cleaning · Staging

A marketplace built for one of this industry's most time-sensitive tasks: preparing a property for the next guest between checkout and check-in. Hosts and property managers post available work, which is claimed by turnover, cleaning, and staging professionals.

For hosts, it's the end of scrambling through group chats at 11 PM hunting for someone to cover a same-day turn. For crews and solopreneurs, it's a direct line to the jobs — no cold outreach, no undercutting, no wondering where the next booking comes from. STRXpo is where both sides of that marketplace will be standing in the same building at the same time, which makes this floor the natural place for it to launch.

Hospitality Guest Consumables

One Vendor · Every Guest Consumable

Snacks, soft drinks, bottled water, coffee pods, and condiments — the items guests expect to find in a well-prepared property, typically sourced from multiple suppliers on separate delivery schedules.

Hospitality Guest Consumables consolidates these items into a single source, selling directly to hosts and property management companies through one order and one invoice.
