Why this exists

The vacation rental industry grew up scattered. Hosts trade advice in Facebook groups. Property managers compare notes on Reddit threads and Slack channels. Software vendors and booking platforms show up wherever the crowd happens to be that week — a real estate conference here, a hospitality trade show there.

None of it was ever built for this industry specifically. STRXpo is.

One room. One floor. Independent hosts scaling their first few listings, property managers running hundreds of doors, the software and service providers that keep it all running, and the platforms everyone lists on — together, for three days, with nothing else competing for the agenda.

That's the whole idea: give an industry that's never had a home base one.
