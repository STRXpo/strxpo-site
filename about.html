The Vacation Rental Industry's First National Trade Show

STRXpo is the first national trade show developed specifically for the vacation rental industry, distinct from general hospitality events and informal online communities. It provides an annual gathering for the professionals driving this industry forward.

The Purpose Behind STRXpo

The vacation rental industry has developed without a centralized professional venue. While the calendar has never been short on regional meetups and general hospitality events, hosts still exchange information through Facebook groups, property managers connect through Reddit and Slack, and software vendors and booking platforms participate in real estate conferences and general hospitality trade shows — without an event created specifically for this industry.

STRXpo was created to fill that gap: a national short-term rental conference and vacation rental trade show built for the people who actually run these businesses.

Most vacation rental industry events ask attendees to choose between education and commerce. STRXpo delivers both. As a combined STR conference and vacation rental expo, it brings independent hosts, property managers, software and service providers, and booking platforms together on a single floor for three days, with an agenda dedicated entirely to this industry.

The exhibit floor represents the full operational stack — vacation rental software providers, turnover and cleaning services, revenue and pricing tools, and the vacation rental technology short-term rental operators depend on daily. The session program delivers short-term rental education from practitioners rather than theorists.

STRXpo is built as a working short-term rental trade show for the entire ecosystem: an Airbnb host conference for independent operators and first-time hosts, a property manager conference for teams scaling their portfolios, a co-host conference for the growing class of managers running properties on behalf of owners, and an Airbnb host networking event where vacation rental property managers, service providers, and platforms meet face to face.

Unlike a regional meetup or a single-track vacation rental conference, STRXpo is designed as a full STR industry conference — one venue, one week each year, and one agenda serving every role this business depends on.

STRXpo establishes a professional home base for an industry that has not previously had one — the short-term rental expo and STR networking event this industry has been operating without.
