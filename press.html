Company Overview

Company Background

STRXpo is the first national trade show built specifically for the vacation rental industry — bringing independent hosts, property managers, cleaning and turnover teams, software providers, and booking platforms together in one place. It's organized by a team of operators with hands-on experience running hosting, turnover, and property management businesses, and produced by Short-Term Rental Expo & Conference Group, LLC.

Using It Correctly

Usage Guidelines

  • The SVG above is vector — it scales to any size (a lapel pin or a stage banner) with zero quality loss. Set the width or height in your design tool; don't stretch it disproportionately.
  • Only the SVG version has a transparent background. The PNG has a solid white background baked in, so it's best reserved for placements on white or very light backgrounds.
  • For additional file sizes or formats not listed here, please contact media@strxpo.com.
